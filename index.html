About

I'm a data scientist, AI/ML researcher, and creative. I love applications of AI that expand the surface area of human creativity, and I strive to make the world a more beautiful and connected place. Relatedly, I love exploring fashion and craft.

For work, I developed the AI/ML pipeline at Lifemine Therapeutics for natural product drug discovery from fungal genomic sequences. I piloted the development of our first fungal foundation model (patent pending). Before that, I started my career in biotech at Ginkgo Bioworks, where I trained models to detect traces of bioengineering.

My other interests include: Victorian literature, great films, French beading, and whimsical objects. My wildest dreams include: directing a movie, designing a fashion line, having a lemon tree in my backyard.

I graduated Magna Cum Laude, Phi Beta Kappa with a degree in Math from Wellesley. My master's thesis at Harvard was on Approximate Bayesian Computation for Parameter Inference and Model Selection in Mechanistic Network Models, advised by J.P. Onnela.

I have a small batch accessories studio featuring whatever I can dream up and execute. My first drop is an oversized Declaration of Independence scarf printed on 100% silk charmeuse and finished with either a simple rolled hem or fringe trim. Check it out at resonancy.studio.

YouShouldDate (formerly DateMyFriend)

I co-founded and scaled the original event series where locals pitch their single friends in the style of a startup pitch deck. We sold out every show across dozens of cities and three countries, including France and Australia. We were featured in The NYTimes, NYPost, CBSN, NPR, Mashable, and more. And yes, several couples have gotten married!

I personally stopped working on this after Covid, but our Paris arm is still functioning under Pitch Dating. I'm happy to see that this has inspired a wave of similar events across the world.

Pomona

My biotech work these days focuses on allergies, the bane of my existence. I was briefly a Product Manager at Pacagen, a consumer allergy startup. Currently, I'm launching a research initiative, Pomona, to explore AI-driven research and development of hypoallergenic food proteins.

We also have a community awareness and advocacy arm. If you suffer from food allergies, whether it's anaphylaxis to peanuts or an itchy mouth from certain fruits or vegetables, stay tuned!
